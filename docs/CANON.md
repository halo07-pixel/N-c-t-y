# Canon reference

What the game follows from the series, and where it is only inspired by it. Everything here is drawn by code
in our own stickman style; no official art, frames or sprites are copied. Facts were checked against public
wiki pages in November 2026; lines marked "check" were not confirmed by a source and are the game's own take.

## Ichigo Kurosaki (swordsman)
| Game skill | Canon basis |
|---|---|
| Getsuga Tenshō (K) | Signature technique of Zangetsu. Shikai: pale blue-white crescent. Tensa Zangetsu (Bankai): black crescent with a red edge (check: colors from common knowledge, not from the fetched page). |
| Getsuga Jūjishō (U) | True Zangetsu: fires two Getsuga Tenshō at once. Drawn as two crescents that cross. |
| Bringer Light (U forward) | Second Fullbring: a high-speed movement technique. Our version is a teleport-slash that hurts along its path. |
| Cero (U down) | Hollow power (Vasto Lorde grants Cero). Red beam with a white-hot core, charged before firing. |
| Bankai (form) | Tensa Zangetsu: black longsword, big speed boost, Getsuga more often. The form already shows the Hollow mask and long hair. |
| Final Getsuga Tenshō (ultimate) | Enhanced Getsuga Tenshō (the Mugetsu form). Drawn black with a red edge. |
| Hollow points (mechanic) | Game mechanic inspired by the Hollow mask power boost; not canon. |

## Uryu Ishida (archer) - research done, not yet applied
- Heilig Pfeil (Holy Arrow): the Quincy's basic spirit arrow, formed from gathered reishi; fired from a bow or other weapons. Colors are not given by the source.
- Licht Regen (Light Rain): gathers reishi along the left arm, then fires a volley of arrows from above at one target. Variant Licht Wind fires horizontally from the ground.
- Ginrei Kojaku: his bow, "shaped like a spider's web". Hirenkyaku: fast movement; a variant makes a reishi platform under the feet.
- Other techniques: Path Control (curved arrows, three at once), Negation of Attacks (an arrow cancels a projectile), Ransōtengai (body manipulation, paralysis), reishi shield, ricochet shots, up to 1,200 consecutive shots.
- Sprenger: not on the Heilig Pfeil page (check before using the name for the trap skill).

## Byakuya Kuchiki (noble) - research done, not yet applied
- Senbonzakura Kageyoshi (Bankai): the sword sinks into the ground and rises as millions of pink cherry-blossom blades controlled by thought.
- Senkei: petals merge into fewer glowing swords in four floating rows around him and the opponent. Ikka Senjinka: all Senkei swords strike at once (the Bankai's ultimate move).
- Gōkei: the blades swirl around the opponent and collapse inward. Shukei: Hakuteiken: the blades condense into one white sword, with white wings and a halo.
- Hadō 4 Byakurai: a white lightning bolt from the forefinger. Hadō 31 Shakkahō: a red energy ball.

## Yhwach (emperor) - research done, not yet applied
- The Almighty (Schrift "A"): sees possible futures as grains of sand and rewrites them; studied abilities lose their power against him; can revive from a future where he survived.
- Auswählen: a pillar of light that destroys a Quincy on contact and redistributes their power.
- Soul Distribution, Schrift (letters that grant abilities), Blut, Hirenkyaku, reishi arrows without a bow, a sword as his standard weapon, overwhelming spiritual pressure (he stole Yamamoto's Bankai).
